About CampusCare

Smart. Simple. Student-First.

CampusCare is the official digital complaint management portal designed to help students report campus issues and receive timely resolutions.

Our Mission

To create a transparent, efficient, and student-friendly platform that bridges the communication gap between students and campus administration, ensuring every complaint is heard, tracked, and resolved.

Our Vision

A campus where every student feels heard and every infrastructure issue is resolved promptly. CampusCare envisions a smarter, more responsive college environment through technology.

How It Works

Students register, submit complaints with category and priority, and receive a unique tracking ID. Admin reviews, updates status, and resolves complaints in an organized manner.
